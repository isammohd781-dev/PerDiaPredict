# Language coverage
The supplied translations.py contains ten languages: English, Arabic, French, Spanish, German, Turkish, Hindi, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.
Medical report and activity report headings support these ten languages, including bundled fonts for Hindi and Chinese and Arabic text shaping. Patient-authored notes are preserved in their original language.
Some newer doctor/admin workflow labels still use English. Full program/privacy policy bodies are available in English, Arabic, Spanish, Hindi and Chinese; other language bodies currently fall back to English. This is a known translation limitation, not a claim of complete ten-language coverage.
